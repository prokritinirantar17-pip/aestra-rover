# <u>PIN LAYOUT</u>

## 1. Battery

| **Battery Terminal** | **Connection** |
|:---|:---|
| **Battery +ve** | Button |
| **Battery -ve** | Two terminals: |
| **i)** | Buck Converter IN- |
| **ii)** | GND power rail (breadboard) |

---

## 2. Buck Converter

### IN PINS

| **Terminal** | **Connection** |
|:---|:---|
| **IN+** | Battery |
| **IN-** | Battery -ve |

### OUT PINS

| **Terminal** | **Connection** |
|:---|:---|
| **OUT+** | 5V power rail (breadboard) |
| **OUT-** | GND power rail (breadboard) |

---

## 3. L298N Module

| **Terminal** | **Connection** |
|:---|:---|
| **12V** | Button |
| **GND** | GND power rail (breadboard) |

---

## 4. Push Button

| **No.** | **Connection** | **Terminal** |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| **1** | Battery | +ve (12V) |
**Two terminals:**
| **2(i)** | Buck converter | IN+ |
| **2(ii)** | L298N Module | +12V |
